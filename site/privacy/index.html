Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026 · covers SplitView 0.3.0 and this website

SplitView has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. The app doesn't send your data to us, and we don't run a server that could receive it.

The app

SplitView doesn't record what you type or what your terminals print. Scrollback is held in memory and goes away when you close the pane. The app doesn't check for updates or contact any service of ours.

These are stored on your computer only, in %APPDATA%\splitview on Windows:

  • Settings (settings.json): colors, font size, aliases, banner text, prompt options, your default shell and panel widths.
  • Configuration (config.toml), if you create one.
  • Files you open in the editor, which are read from and saved to your own disk.

Programs you run inside SplitView, such as git or npm, make their own network connections. Those are outside SplitView's control.

Remote control

Remote control is off by default, and nothing listens on your network unless you turn it on in Settings. The phone app isn't available yet. When you enable the feature:

  • SplitView creates a self-signed certificate and private key.
  • Your computer's name and the server's port are announced on your local network over mDNS, so a phone can find it.
  • In %APPDATA%\splitview\remote, SplitView keeps the certificate and key, a list of paired devices with hashed access tokens, and an audit log. The log records events such as enabling the server, pairing attempts and connections, with details like device names, addresses and pairing codes.
  • A paired device can see your pane titles, working directories and terminal output, and can type into your panes. That traffic is encrypted and goes directly between the device and your computer, not through us.

You can revoke a device in Settings, which also ends its live session. Delete the remote folder to remove the stored data.
